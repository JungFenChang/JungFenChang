### 🍷 Featured Project: [Wine Expert](https://github.com/JungFenChang/wine_expert_ai)

This is an integrated AI project that combines large language models (LLMs), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and traditional machine learning (ML) techniques to provide professional services for wine quality prediction, flavor classification and knowledge querying
- **[Data Research & Insights] [wine insights](https://github.com/JungFenChang/wine_insights_da)**： The analytical foundation of this project. It involves **EDA** and **Data Engineering**, including strategic outlier handling (comparing **StandardScaler** vs. **MinMaxScaler**) and **Unsupervised Learning** to define distinct wine profiles.
  
- **Quality Prediction & Explainable Analysis (ML & XAI)**：By integrating pre-trained prediction models with prompt engineering, the LLM produces detailed analytical reports on wine quality from the perspective of chemical characteristics.
- **Flavor Feature Clustering (Clustering)**：Using unsupervised learning, the system automatically identifies flavor attributes from the chemical features of wine, and—through prompt design—uses the LLM to provide descriptive explanations of those flavors.
- **Intelligent Knowledge Q&A System (RAG)**：A professional wine knowledge base is built to mitigate hallucinations in large language models and to deliver accurate answers supported by reliable sources.
- **Localized Expert Model (Fine-tuned)**：Demonstrating a complete pipeline, the project covers fine-tuning, GGUF quantization, and local deployment with Ollama.
